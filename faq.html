FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I deposit?

Open Deposit, choose MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, use the official payment details shown there, then submit the exact amount, sender number and SMS transaction ID for verification.

How do I withdraw?

Open Withdraw, enter the amount, select your Mobile Money network and receiver number, review the applicable fee and submit the request for approval.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Withdrawal requests are reviewed according to the platform's configured processing process. Check your notification for the latest status.

Why is my deposit pending?

A pending deposit is waiting for verification. Keep your Mobile Money confirmation SMS until the request is approved or rejected.

How do machines work?

Machines are configured by MDH administrators. Open Machines to view available series, machine information, pricing and configured returns.

What happens if my account is suspended?

Restricted financial and machine actions may be disabled. Check your account notification for the reason and use support or Mara AI for guidance.

How do I reset my password?

Open Settings and use Reset Password. Follow the secure recovery instructions provided by the authentication system.

How do referrals work?

Open Referral to view your referral code, link, team information and configured referral earnings.
