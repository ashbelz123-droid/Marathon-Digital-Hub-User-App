Privacy Policy

How MDH handles account information

Information We Use

Marathon Digital Hub may use account details such as your name, email, phone number, profile information, wallet activity, machine activity, deposits, withdrawals, referrals and notifications to provide the requested services.

Authentication

Authentication data is handled through the configured authentication system. Never share your password, reset link or verification code with another person.

Payments

Payment and transaction information submitted through the platform is used to process and verify deposit and withdrawal requests.

Storage

Profile and machine-related images may be stored using configured Supabase Storage buckets. Access should follow the platform's configured security policies.

Security

We use authentication, database access controls and validation to protect account and financial information. Users should also protect their own credentials and devices.

Notifications

Account-specific notifications may be generated for deposits, withdrawals, income, machine activity, referrals, security events and platform announcements.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact official Marathon Digital Hub support.
